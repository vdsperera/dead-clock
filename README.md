# 🕰️ DEAD CLOCK

*Twelve everyday questions. Nothing spooky in them — but you won't have time to give the answer you'd like to give. Just the one that's actually true.*

**Dead Clock** is a minimalist, browser-based psychological game that tests your gut instincts under extreme time pressure. 

You are presented with 12 slice-of-life scenarios and two choices. The catch? The clock is ticking fast, and it doesn't wait for you to come up with the "right" answer. At the end of the game, your split-second decisions are analyzed to generate a personalized profile based on four axes: **Honesty**, **Control**, **Presence**, and **Connection**.

## ✨ Features
* **Intense Time Pressure:** Choose your difficulty, from "Calm" (3.2 seconds) to "Dead" (1.0 second).
* **Dynamic Time Management:** Need more time? Hit the panic button `[3]` to sacrifice points for a few extra seconds.
* **Psychological Profiling:** A custom end-game report card that visualizes your decision-making tendencies.
* **Atmospheric UI:** A gritty, dark-mode aesthetic with custom animations, SVG clock graphics, and immersive fonts.

## 🚀 How to Play
1. Open `index.html` in your web browser.
2. Select your difficulty timer.
3. Press `[ENTER]` to wind the clock.
4. Use your mouse or keyboard (`[1]` for choice A, `[2]` for choice B, `[3]` for extra time) to answer before the clock shatters.
