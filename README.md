# 🕰️ DEAD CLOCK

*Twelve everyday scenarios. Nothing spooky in them — but you won't have time to give the answer you'd like to give. Just the one that's actually true. The clock does not wait for you to invent a better self.*

**Dead Clock** is an atmospheric, browser-based psychological game that tests your gut instincts under extreme time pressure. 

You are presented with 12 slice-of-life dilemmas and two choices. The catch? The clock is ticking fast, and it doesn't wait for you to deliberate. At the end of the run, your split-second decisions and hesitations generate a personalized **Psychological Profile Dossier** across four bipolar axes: **Honesty**, **Control**, **Presence**, and **Connection**.

---

## ✨ Features

* **Procedural Web Audio Atmosphere:** 100% synthesized sound design (mechanical ticking that accelerates under duress, sub-bass heartbeat pulses, glass fracture chimes on freezes, and typewriter clicks) using the native Web Audio API — zero external assets required.
* **Intense Time Pressure:** Choose your difficulty timer, from "Frozen" (8.0s) down to "Dead" (1.0s).
* **Red-Zone Panic Mode:** Dynamic peripheral crimson vignettes and needle tremors when time drops below 30%.
* **Emergency Lifeline:** Hit `[3]` or `[Space]` to borrow critical seconds when a dilemma stops you in your tracks.
* **Psychological Profile Dossier:** Unlocks one of 16 personality archetypes (e.g. *The Relentless Realist*, *The Calculated Witness*, *The Intuitive Diplomat*) with personalized narrative breakdowns.
* **Bipolar Spectrum Visualizer:** Dual-sided trait sliders illustrating where your instincts fall on each psychological spectrum.
* **Dilemma Hesitation Heatmap:** Automatically identifies the exact scenario that caused your deepest hesitation or freeze.
* **1-Click Viral Dossier Sharing:** Formats and copies your test results directly to your clipboard for sharing on Discord, Twitter/X, and Reddit.
* **Occult Dark Aesthetic:** Ambient canvas dust particles, SVG clockwork animations, antique typography, and responsive mobile-ready layout.

## 🚀 How to Play & Controls

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser.
2. Select your difficulty speed and default extra time boost.
3. Press `[ENTER]` or click **WIND IT** to start.
4. **Controls:**
   * **Choice A:** `[1]`, `[A]`, `[← Left Arrow]`, or Mouse Click
   * **Choice B:** `[2]`, `[B]`, `[→ Right Arrow]`, or Mouse Click
   * **Emergency Lifeline (+Extra Time):** `[3]`, `[Space]`, or Mouse Click
   * **Toggle Sound:** `[M]` or Top HUD Button
   * **Toggle Fullscreen:** `[F]` or Top HUD Button
   * **Restart Run:** `[R]` or `[ENTER]` on End Screen
   * **Copy Dossier:** `[C]` or Button on End Screen
